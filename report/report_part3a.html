3. Features, split and preprocessing

3.1 Selected features, and why each is valid at the pre-race cutoff

We built 21 candidate features in five groups and evaluated each in the EDA (Section 3.3). The models use the 18 that show a signal; the three with none (pit_lane_start, home_race, field_size) are not model inputs (D30), and Section 4.6 checks that adding them back never helps. The groups are reused by the ablation. Every feature is known after qualifying and before the start.

Raw lap times are not used, because they partly measure track length (Monaco ≈ 70 s, Spa ≈ 105 s); a gap inside one race normalises circuit, season and weather at once, like the FIA's 107% rule (D15). 3.2 Leakage controls

  • Standings from before the race. At the 2023 Belgian GP, Verstappen's standings row says 314 points, which already includes that race. The feature uses the 281 he had before it. An assertion checks that every prev_raceId lies strictly earlier in time than its race.
  • Form from past races only. shift(1) keeps the current race out of its own window, and races are ordered by date, not by raceId. A hand check recomputes Verstappen's podium rate over the 5 races before race 989 from the raw rows (0.40 = feature, asserted).
  • No post-race column in the features. An assertion checks every candidate feature against all post-race columns: positionOrder, points, laps, status, finishing rank, mechanical flag, and the rest.
  • Choices on training data only. Feature evidence, the 20% gap cutoff and every preprocessing statistic come from the training seasons.
